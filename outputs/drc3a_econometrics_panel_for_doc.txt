Econometric results panel and interpretation guide

How to read this panel: OR > 1 means higher odds; OR < 1 means lower odds. AUC=0.5 is chance, about 0.7 is useful, and about 0.8 is strong. Cramer V around 0.1 is weak and around 0.3 is moderate. Significance marks: *** p<0.001, ** p<0.01, * p<0.05, ~ p<0.10, n.s. not statistically clear. These are exploratory cross-sectional tests, so the interpretation should be associational rather than causal.

Summary read: the strongest and most stable result is generator presence predicting oxygen access and concentrator access. Energy source count, energy profile, and grid architecture also pattern oxygen outcomes, but architecture predicts reconstructed reliability only modestly. Bad-power and outage measures are most useful as mechanism indicators because they predict reported equipment damage, including oxygen devices.
