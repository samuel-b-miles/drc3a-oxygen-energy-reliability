## Team Update Draft

Today I set up a cleaner DRC3 working environment in Drive and started converting the oxygen manuscript materials into a reproducible manuscript pipeline. The current DRC3a concept is a high-N/high-T paper: the DRC2 survey gives the broad facility-level picture of energy and oxygen regimes, while DRC1/Valentin/Bethesda monitoring gives the longitudinal evidence that electricity regimes are temporal, fragile, human-operated, and clinically consequential.

The most useful early finding is from the literature mapping: oxygen research is rich on clinical oxygen need, mortality, oximetry, and solar oxygen, but thinner on measured electricity as an oxygen-system variable. DRC3a can step into that gap by treating oxygen access as a critical health-facility electrification problem, not just as equipment availability.

The next decision is journal/authorship strategy. A Nature Energy version would foreground energy reliability, health facility electrification, and oxygen as a tracer load; that may point toward Dan as final author. A Lancet Global Health version would foreground oxygen security, health systems, and mortality/readiness; that may point toward Layla as last author. I want to weigh this carefully with everyone before locking the framing.
