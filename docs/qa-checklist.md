# DRC3 QA Checklist

## Data

- Raw inputs are preserved and never overwritten.
- Source file, Drive URL, date received, and transformation script are recorded.
- Facility IDs and health-zone names are standardized.
- Missingness and outlier handling are documented.
- Any sensitive fields are removed or redacted before sharing.

## Analysis

- Each output figure is generated by a named script.
- Figure inputs are stored as processed tables.
- Classification rules are written down before interpretation.
- Literature coding has a manual-review flag.
- High-N survey results and high-T monitoring results are kept analytically distinct before synthesis.

## Manuscript

- Claims are traceable to figures, tables, or cited literature.
- Nature Energy framing and Lancet Global Health framing are tracked as separate strategic branches until the team decides.
- Authorship notes are treated as planning material, not final decisions.
- Submission checklist is created once target journal is chosen.
